# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a lightweight, iterative lifecycle. Teams initiate work by validating the business need, stakeholders, measurable outcomes, and go/no-go decision. They plan approved work into prioritized, estimable increments with clear acceptance criteria, ownership, dependencies, risks, and a Definition of Done. During execution, the team tracks work on a project board, uses small reviewed pull requests, runs automated checks, and shares regular delivery updates. Releases require quality and security checks, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and post-deployment verification. Teams then capture learnings in retrospectives and track improvement actions. Throughout the lifecycle, they monitor risks and success metrics, communicate with stakeholders, and escalate blockers through agreed paths.
